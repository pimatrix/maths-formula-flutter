Features

Not just formulas — the whole understanding

A formula you don't understand is useless in the exam hall. Every entry in MathMitra explains what the formula is for, what each symbol means, and how to use it on a real question.

Complete formula library

291 formulas across basics and Maths-I Units 1–5 — each with its meaning, symbol-by-symbol explanation and a solved example.

AI Tutor

Ask any maths doubt and get a clear, step-by-step solution. Open it from any formula to ask about that formula specifically.

Photo scan

Photograph a formula from your notebook or textbook — the AI reads it and drafts the entry for you to save.

Full PDF in one tap

Download the complete formula book as a clean PDF for last-minute revision — or to print and keep in your file.

Light, dark & system theme

A calm graph-paper design that switches with your phone, or pick the look you like and it stays.

Your account, everywhere

Sign up free to add your own formulas and categories. The same account works on the app and the website.

AI Tutor

Stuck at midnight? Ask the Tutor.

Type a question — an equation, a doubt from class, a step you don't follow — and get a patient, step-by-step answer in simple English. Free questions are included every day, and if you ever need more, bring your own key.

Try the AI Tutor
  • 10 free questions a day on the app's own AI — resets at midnight.
  • Unlimited with your own key: paste a free Google Gemini key and the limit disappears.
  • Your key stays yours: it is saved only on your device, never sent to our server, never stored in our database — the app talks to Google directly.
  • Formula-aware: ask from any formula page and the Tutor answers about that formula.
